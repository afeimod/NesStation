Ishiiruka (Dolphin fork) — GameCube / Wii emulator core
=======================================================

Ishiiruka-Dolphin is a performance-oriented fork of the Dolphin emulator
(by Tinob), itself licensed under GPLv2+.

Licensed under GPLv2 (or any later version).
https://github.com/Tinob/Ishiiruka

The NesStation integration vendors the upstream Android JNI host contract
(org.dolphinemu.ishiiruka.NativeLibrary and supporting classes) and loads
the prebuilt libmain.so (renamed libishiiruka.so) extracted from the
Ishiruka Android APK via scripts/fetch_azahar_ishiruka_libs.sh. The
prebuilt core remains under its upstream license. You must comply with
GPLv2 when distributing derivatives.
