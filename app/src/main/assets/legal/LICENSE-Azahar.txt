Azahar / AzaharPlus — Nintendo 3DS emulator core
=================================================

Azahar is a free and open-source Nintendo 3DS emulator, continuing the
legacy of Citra and Lime3DS.

Licensed under GPLv2 (or any later version).
https://github.com/azahar-emu/azahar
https://github.com/AzaharPlus/AzaharPlus (extra-features fork)

The NesStation integration vendors the upstream Android JNI host contract
(org.citra.citra_emu.NativeLibrary and supporting stub classes) and loads
the prebuilt libcitra-android.so (renamed libazahar.so) extracted from the
AzaharPlus APK via scripts/fetch_azahar_ishiruka_libs.sh. The prebuilt core
remains under its upstream license. You must comply with GPLv2 when
distributing derivatives.
